THE PERSON BEHIND THE PAGE

“Some things are easier to understand when left unsaid.”

Born in Vancouver and now living in Tokyo, Griffin Eaves is an English-language writer whose books are currently being translated into a second language, bringing his work to new readers beyond the English-speaking world.

His fiction finds the extraordinary in the quiet corners of life: the spaces between strangers, the weight of unspoken words, distant cities and fleeting encounters that linger long after they end.
